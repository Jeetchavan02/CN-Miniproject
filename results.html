Key Takeaways from the Data

Resilience Under Loss

At 5% packet loss, HTTP/1.1 load times surge by 289% and HTTP/2 by 313% due to TCP head-of-line blocking. HTTP/3 load times only increase by 26%.

Handshake Economy

QUIC reduces handshake overhead by ~86% compared to HTTP/1.1 (45ms vs 320ms) because transport negotiation and TLS 1.3 keys exchange simultaneously.

Clean Fiber Equality

On zero-loss, low-RTT connections (e.g. gigabit fiber), HTTP/2 multiplexing achieves parity with HTTP/3. QUIC's greatest edge is on mobile and lossy links.
